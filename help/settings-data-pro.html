Settings and ownership

Keep DKReminder predictable, portable, and under your control.

Settings define app-wide defaults. Backup and restore protect your local data. Pro adds advanced creation and automation, but losing Pro never deletes your reminders.

Find the right setting

Open Settings from the main navigation. Changes here become defaults for the app; a reminder can still have its own supported schedule or alert choices.

App language

Follow the system language or choose a supported interface language.

Reminder permissions

Review notification, exact-alarm, and full-screen access needed for dependable alerts. See what each permission changes.

Reminder creation

Choose whether the + button opens step-by-step creation or the full form. Step-by-step presentation is Pro; the full form remains available.

Snooze

Set the default delay and the minute options shown when you snooze. See how they are used during an alert.

Repeat alerts without a response

Enable app-wide follow-up alerts, their interval, and additional count. Compare global and reminder-specific choices.

Calendar exceptions

The app-wide Month overflow rule decides what happens when a shorter month does not have the selected day: use the last day of that month, use the first day of the next month, or skip the month. For reminders set to the 29th–31st, the editor can follow this rule or use a reminder-specific choice. This section also sets the app-wide rule for February 29.

Alert mode

Choose the app-wide alert presentation. Android permissions can still limit the result. Compare all four choices.

Sound

Choose the default sound, manual-cycle start sound, and consecutive playback count for one alert.

Global pause

Pause every reminder. Occurrences skipped during the pause are not recreated automatically when you resume.

After global pause

Paused alerts do not restart automatically when global pause is turned off; handle them separately in Active.

Catalog

Restore the default favorite presets. This replaces the current favorite selection and order, not your reminders.

Export a backup or restore one

Create a backup

  1. Open Settings → Backup.
  2. Choose Export data.
  3. Select a safe destination and keep the resulting file private.
  4. Wait for Backup saved before leaving the app.

Restore from a file

  1. Before restoring, export the current state if you might need it later.
  2. Choose Import data and select a DKReminder backup.
  3. Read the replacement warning, then choose Replace data.
  4. Wait for the success message and review the next scheduled reminders.
Restore replaces all current local data and cannot be undone. A backup made by a newer DKReminder version is rejected safely; update the app and try again.

Know what Exchange restore does not restart

A backup preserves supported local reminder data and historical transfer annotations. It does not contain account passwords or turn transfer history into a live account session.

  • After installation on another device, active queued, sending, or failed outbound operations are not restored as automatically runnable sends.
  • Sent history and received-origin annotations may remain as history, without credentials or tokens.
  • Exchange account state, current permissions, Inbox, and server records are refreshed after you sign in; the local backup is not their source of truth.
  • Old compatible backup versions receive safe defaults for fields that did not exist then. A newer unknown format is rejected before replacing data.
After moving devices: sign in to Exchange separately, refresh Inbox, Sent, and Permissions, then inspect any restored transfer-related record before acting. Do not assume an old queued send resumed.

Understand the first 30 days of Pro

All current Pro features are available for 30 × 24 hours after the first successful launch of a Pro-enabled build. This is initial access, not a Google Play free trial.

  • No payment method is required.
  • No subscription starts automatically and there is no automatic charge when this access ends.
  • The Pro screen shows the end date, and Pro choices are marked PRO from the first day.
  • Restoring a backup restores the previous initial-access date; it does not create another 30 days.

Subscribe, manage, or restore a purchase

DKReminder Pro offers monthly and annual auto-renewing subscription plans through Google Play. The current localized prices and billing periods come from Google Play.

  1. Open Settings → DKReminder Pro.
  2. If the plans are shown on this screen, select one before choosing Subscribe or Change plan. If that button opens a plan-selection window, choose your plan there.
  3. Review the selected plan's price and renewal terms, then continue to Google Play. Check the plan, price, and billing date in Google Play before confirming.
  4. Use Manage subscription to view subscription details or cancel renewal in Google Play.

The selected plan applies to Subscribe or Change plan. Manage subscription opens Google Play separately and does not carry over that selection. The selected option is not proof of your current plan; check the subscription details in Google Play.

Canceling renewal does not remove Pro immediately: access continues while Google Play reports the subscription as active. A pending payment does not activate paid access.

Restore purchases

On the Pro screen, choose Restore purchases. DKReminder asks Google Play for the current purchase state; it does not create a new charge. Use the same Google Play account that owns the subscription and wait for the result.

If your version shows Purchase status updated, the check succeeded, but that message alone does not mean an active subscription was found. A result saying no active subscription was found refers to Google Play purchases, not your initial or complimentary Pro access. Restore does not restart a canceled subscription. After a subscription ends, Google Play may report that there is no subscription to manage.

What happens after Pro access ends

Your reminders, history, structured scenario data, and saved Pro choices remain. Basic reminders, the full editor, alerts, actions, trash, presets, backup, and restore keep working.

  • You cannot enable a new Pro feature or edit its Pro-owned parameters.
  • Pro runtime behavior is paused where silently falling back would change the reminder's meaning.
  • Basic fields can still be edited, and a Pro choice can be changed explicitly to a basic alternative.
  • When access returns, preserved choices become available again and future schedules are reconciled from now; missed Pro events are not replayed in a burst.
No silent data loss: locked does not mean deleted. Open a reminder's details to see preserved values and the effective behavior while Pro is unavailable.

Privacy, local data, and account deletion

Open Settings → Legal and privacy → Privacy Policy, or read the public Privacy Policy. Treat exported backup files as private because they contain your local app data.

The optional Exchange account is separate from local reminders. Deleting it removes the Exchange profile and server-side account records, but not local reminders on your device or independent copies already saved by recipients. Review the account deletion instructions first.

Troubleshooting

The backup cannot be imported

Confirm it is an unmodified DKReminder backup. If it was created by a newer app, update DKReminder. An unsupported or damaged file is rejected without intentionally replacing current data.

Pro is not restored

Check that Google Play uses the account that purchased the subscription, reconnect, then choose Restore purchases. If Google Play is temporarily unavailable, the last successfully confirmed matching access may remain until status can refresh.

The + button no longer opens step-by-step creation

Step-by-step presentation requires Pro. Your preference remains saved, but without access the + button opens the full form. Restore Pro or continue with the full editor.

Alerts changed after Pro ended

Open the reminder details and look for its PRO-marked setting. DKReminder preserves the value but may stop Pro scheduling or use an explicit documented app-wide basic setting rather than silently changing stored data.
