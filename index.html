Reminder exchange

Send a complete reminder. The recipient decides what happens next.

Exchange is optional. Local reminders still work without an account. When you choose Exchange, DKReminder sends the reminder you prepared — not a shared task and not a chat message.

1

Permission first

Sending permission is one-way and must be approved by the recipient. Either person can revoke it later.

2

An independent copy

The recipient can reject the item or open a filled editor, change anything, and save a new local reminder.

3

A private outcome

The sender cannot control the recipient’s copy and does not learn whether it was opened, imported, completed, or deleted.

Prepare the reminder once, then choose Save, Send, or Save and send. The recipient is selected by their exact verified Exchange email address; DKReminder has no public people directory.

Requesting permission and sending require PRO. Signing in, approving, receiving, rejecting, and creating a local copy do not.

Privacy

Local by default, cloud only when you choose Exchange.

Local reminders stay on your device. An optional Exchange account uses Firebase only for account operations and reminders you explicitly send. Google Play handles the Pro subscription. DKReminder has no ads or analytics SDKs.
